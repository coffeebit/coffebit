Before You Reach Out

Is Coffebit Right For You?

We'd rather be honest upfront than waste your time. Here's what you should know.

Do you work with very small budgets?

Our minimum project scope is around $5,000 USD. We build systems that last — not minimum viable mockups. If you need a quick landing page, we're not the right fit.

How fast can you start?

Typically within two weeks of a signed scope. We schedule architecture reviews within 48 hours of your first message. We don't have a long intake queue.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Our systems are built to international standards. We work with teams across the region and further abroad, fully remote. Time-zone alignment is discussed upfront.

What if I already have a team or an existing codebase?

We offer audits, architecture consulting, and embedded engineering — not just greenfield builds. We can work alongside your existing team or take over a struggling project.
